The Experience

Five Nights Horror is an independent game project from Andronex Studio. Its goal is to create a tense, atmospheric horror experience with mysterious surroundings and strategic gameplay.

The project is inspired by the broader animatronic survival-horror genre while developing its own presentation and ideas.

Planned Highlights

🌑

Atmospheric Horror

A dark environment designed to build tension through sound, lighting and suspense.

👁️

Observe Carefully

A gameplay direction centred on paying attention to your surroundings and making decisions.

🔒

Survival Challenges

Planned challenges that reward careful thinking and strategic resource use.
