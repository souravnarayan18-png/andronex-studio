THE ORIGINAL AI PROJECT

Where it all began.

Every ambitious idea has a starting point. SouravAI was the original AI assistant project that began the journey toward Andronex's evolving vision for intelligent technology.

AN EXPERIMENT THAT STARTED A JOURNEY

The Beginning of an Idea

SouravAI emerged from an interest in building a personal AI assistant rather than simply relying on existing applications.

Through experimentation with AI models, conversational interfaces and different development tools, the project became a foundation for exploring more personalised and capable AI experiences.

💬 Conversational AI

Exploring natural conversations that help users ask questions, understand concepts and develop ideas.

🧪 Experimentation

Testing AI models, prompts and integrations to understand their strengths, limitations and practical applications.

🧠 Personalisation

Exploring the possibility of an assistant that can adapt its responses to user preferences and conversational context.

🚀 A Foundation for Growth

Lessons from SouravAI helped shape the broader vision behind the next generation of Andronex AI development.

From SouravAI to Orvyn

SouravAI represents the beginning of the journey. Orvyn is the more ambitious successor project, exploring a broader combination of conversational intelligence, personalisation, memory and research tools.

The two projects are connected by a shared goal: exploring how AI can become a more useful and accessible everyday assistant.

Project Status

SouravAI is the original project in the Andronex AI development journey. Its available features depend on the current implementation. Orvyn continues to develop the broader vision that grew from this beginning.
