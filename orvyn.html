Built Around a Bigger Vision

Exploring how different AI capabilities can work together in one accessible assistant.

Orvyn is an independent AI assistant project developed under Andronex Studio. Its broader vision is to move beyond isolated question-and-answer interactions and explore a more continuous, contextual and personalised AI experience.

Through experimentation with language models, memory systems, research tools and software architecture, Orvyn aims to explore new possibilities in everyday AI assistance.

A More Personal AI Experience

Orvyn explores a recognisable conversational personality, contextual responses and adaptable communication. The goal is not simply to imitate human behaviour, but to create an approachable assistant that remains useful, transparent and focused on the user's needs.

From SouravAI to Orvyn

Every ambitious project has an origin.

Orvyn grew from the experimentation behind SouravAI, the original AI assistant project that began this journey. Working with AI models, prompts and software tools helped shape a broader vision for a more personalised and versatile assistant.

SouravAI represents the beginning. Orvyn represents the next stage of that evolving vision.
